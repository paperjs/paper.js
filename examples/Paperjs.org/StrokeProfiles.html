Stroke Profiles

Drag out from the stroke to add a stop. Drag the round handle of a stop to change its width and angle, with Shift only its width, the square one to change its aspect, and the stop itself to move it along the path. Alt-drag copies a stop, Delete removes the selected one. Drag the path's points and handles: the stops stay between the same segments. Draw a new path in the empty space. Zoom with the scroll wheel, pan with the space bar held.

Faded parts of a stop are values it takes from the stops around it, and grey values in the fields too; changing them makes them its own.

The area that the pen sweeps is the path's outline(), and the stops are drawn over its selection through onDrawSelection.

logs the path and its profile to the console, and copies them.
