Tool Effects

The Fancy Brush tool, as a live effect: its tool draws along each stroke as a hand would, faster on straights and slower in turns, with the tool's own handlers. The strokes stay plain paths: draw new ones with the mouse, drag their points, handles or the strokes themselves to edit them, and change the brush of the selected one here: its size, as a factor of the stroke width, its stroke ends, and the hand's speed, grip in turns and acceleration.

Curvature shows the curvature of a stroke as Glyphs does: a comb outside each turn, as wide as the curvature and colored by it, which also shows where it jumps at the segments.
